The full project repository is available at
- https://github.com/NataKrj/Automated-Risk-Scoring-System.
It contains:
/1_data_synthesis/ – data synthesis methods, generators, and scenario specifications
/2_scenario_catalogue/ – AML scenarios based on FI and EU guidance
/3_data_quality/ – rule-based validation and feature profiling
/4_model_selection/ – AutoML experiments, feature importance
/5_class_balance/ – class-imbalance handling (SMOTE, ADASYN, ROSE)
/6_training_adaptation/ – model training logic and binary and multiclass results
/meeting_minutes/ – meeting minutes with the supervisor
/artefacts/ – supporting materials (presentations, Gantt chart, conference materials, Zotero library)
